# Fearless Focus

Follow the book *Fearless Focus* with Brainstorm. The book shows you how to set goals you really want, face the fears and risks that stand in the way, and build the daily and weekly habits that keep you moving. This sample turns it into a plan that reminds you, repeats, and keeps a record of your progress.

This series is for **readers**. You do not need to design anything: the structure (`fearless-focus.bss`) is ready, and each episode fills in a plan one part of the book at a time. The plan is the author's own, Kelly Salvage, so you can see a real plan grow alongside yours.

## Episodes

| Episode | File | Read first | What is added to the plan |
|---------|------|------------|---------------------------|
| 0 | `00-program.bsd` | The introduction | The program as it is published: the routine, the daily and weekly reviews with their guides, and the monthly assessment |
| 1 | `01-where-i-am.bsd` | Chapter 1 | The program started, and a first assessment of where I am |
| 2 | `02-goals.bsd` | Chapters 3 and 4 | Three goals in order of priority, and the reasons behind them |
| 3 | `03-make-them-yours.bsd` | Chapters 1 and 5 | Who I want to be, how each goal will feel, and old dreams achieved, laid to rest or on hold |
| 4 | `04-vision-and-affirmation.bsd` | Chapters 6 and 7 | A vision board and a focus affirmation |
| 5 | `05-action-plan.bsd` | Chapters 8 and 9 | Milestones, actions with the outcomes I expect, and regular habits with schedules |
| 6 | `06-fears-and-risks.bsd` | Chapters 13 to 17 | Fears, obstacles and doubts, measured as risks |
| 7 | `07-controls.bsd` | Chapter 18 | Controls and their tasks, each risk rated again, and the risks I accept |
| 8 | `08-reviews.bsd` | Chapters 10 and 11 | The end of the first day: the routine done, and the first evening review and weekly planning |

Each file is the complete plan at the end of that episode, so comparing two episodes shows exactly what was added.

## Following along

1. Import `fearless-focus.bss` as a structure.
2. Import `00-program.bsd`, and start it. This gives you your own plan, with your routine starting tomorrow.
3. Work through the book, filling in your plan as each episode does. Episodes 1 to 8 show how my plan looked at each point, if you want to compare.

The full language is described in the [README](../../README.md) at the top of this repository.
