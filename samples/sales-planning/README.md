# Sales planning

Build a sales plan from scratch: a target for the quarter, the clients you are selling to, the deals in your pipeline, the follow-ups that win them, and totals that tell you how far you are from the target.

This series is for **authors**: people who want to design their own structures. It follows on from the project planning series and adds choices, calculated values, schedules and totals. Each step adds one idea to the structure (`.bss`) and uses it in a brainstorm document (`.bsd`). Each step's files are complete, so you can open any step on its own, and comparing two steps shows exactly what changed.

## Steps

| Step | Files | What you build | What you learn |
|------|-------|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | `01-target` | A plan for the quarter with a target | Expected values, rules and conditions |
| 2 | `02-clients` | Clients, each with a main contact | Links to contacts |
| 3 | `03-deals` | Deals that move through stages | Choices and default values |
| 4 | `04-forecast` | A probability and weighted value for each deal | Calculated values, and adding elements at the end of a type |
| 5 | `05-follow-ups` | Next steps, including a check-in every other Friday | Extending TaskItem, and schedules |
| 6 | `06-activities` | A log of calls, emails and meetings | Default values: `now` and `me` |
| 7 | `07-totals` | Won, forecast, deals won and the gap to target | Totals over lists |

## Following along

For each step:
1. Import the step's `.bss` file as a structure.
2. Import the step's `.bsd` file in the default import mode, so that the contacts are created for you. It names the step's structure in its front matter.
3. Open the brainstorm, and look at the clients, the deals and "My tasks".

When you import a later step, choose to replace the brainstorm from the step before.

The full language is described in the [README](../../README.md) at the top of this repository.
